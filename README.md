# Employly Connect (59)

Build a web app for my company "Employly". The main page lets both, those who are "Hiring" (small local businesses) and those who are "Seeking" (people searching for jobs, willing to work for small salaries) do this one thing: they each have a profile where they can add their names, they can add their interests/requirements, they can add what they are looking for, they can add their salary expectations (for job finders)/how much salary they are willing to pay (for employers). Use these colours: navy blue, beige. Keep it simple and easy to read on a phone.

There should be two roles and an easy way to switch between the two. Add these features to the two roles: Hiring and Seeking

for both (Hiring and Seeking): -they have their homepage, there will be tabs on the top which they can select one or more, the tabs are related to talents and skills (crochet, baking, knitting, painting, drawing, social media, flyers making, pottery, etc.)

for "Hiring": -when they select the tabs, profiles of people with those specific talents and skills will appear on their homepage, and when they click on their profile they can see all the information they input in their profiles
-if they feel like the person has similar talents as what the business needs, they can click a button to request the person to enroll as an employee in their business

for "Seeking": -when they selct the tabs, profile of small local businesses with those specific areas will appear on their homepage, and when they click on the business profile, they can see all their qualifications and what type of employees they are looking for, etc.
-if they feel like the small business has similar requirements and offers what they want, they can click a button to request employment in their business
